# Limitations

- Injury reports after 2024 are not automatically ingested.
- Rookie enrichment without `CFBD_API_KEY` uses reduced draft/combine features.
- Fixture exports must never be treated as real rankings.
- Models estimate next-season statistics; they do not guarantee outcomes.
- Mid-week roster news after the last refresh is not automatic.
- Advanced datasets (NGS, PFR, participation) have incomplete historical coverage.
- COVID-era and schedule-length changes distort some season totals.
